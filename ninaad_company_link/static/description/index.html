Overview

Create Company from Contact

Many businesses start with a single company and grow over time. When it is time to expand, a new company often has to be set up for an organisation that already exists as a contact in Odoo. In standard Odoo, the company has to be created separately and linked to its contact by hand.

The Create Company from Contact module adds a Create Company action to the Contact form. With one click, it turns an existing company contact into a new Odoo company and links the contact to it, so the same record is used in Contacts and in Settings → Companies.

The new company is also added to the allowed companies of the user who creates it, so it can be selected in the company switcher right away.

Features

  • One-click company creation: a new Create Company action is added to the Actions (gear) menu of the Contact form.
  • Existing contact is reused: the contact is set as the Partner of the new company. No duplicate contact is created.
  • The company takes its name from the contact, and the contact's address, UEN / TRN and other details stay in one place.
  • Duplicate protection: if a company already exists for the contact, no second company is created.
  • Works for contacts of type Company. Individual contacts are skipped.
  • Automatic access: the new company is added to the allowed companies of the current user.
  • The action can be run on several selected contacts from the list view at the same time.
  • The action is available to Administrators only (Settings group).

Configuration

STEP 1 — OPEN THE EXISTING CONTACT

Go to Contacts and open the company contact you want to expand with a new company.

  • The contact must be of type Company.
  • Make sure the name, address, and other details are correct. The new company uses this contact.
STEP 2 — CLICK CREATE COMPANY

Click the Actions (gear icon) next to the contact name and select Create Company.

  • A new company is created with the same name as the contact.
  • The existing contact is linked as the Partner of the new company.
  • If a company already exists for this contact, nothing is created again.
  • The action is visible to users with the Administration: Settings access right.
STEP 3 — CHECK THE NEW COMPANY

Go to Settings → Users & Companies → Companies.

  • The new company is listed, with the contact shown in the Contact field.
  • Address, UEN, TRN and other details come from the linked contact.
  • The company is added to your allowed companies, so you can select it in the company switcher at the top right.
  • Set the currency, branches and other company settings as needed.
